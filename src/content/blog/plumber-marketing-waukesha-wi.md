If you're a plumber in Waukesha, you already know the deal: word of mouth is great, but it's unpredictable. One slow season can hurt. And every time a homeowner searches "plumber near me" and calls someone else, that's a job you didn't get — not because you're worse, but because they found the other guy first.

Here's what actually moves the needle for plumbing businesses in Waukesha County.

## 1. Google Business Profile Is Your #1 Tool

When someone's toilet is overflowing at 9pm, they're not scrolling Facebook ads. They're going to Google, typing "plumber Waukesha WI," and calling the first business that looks legit.

The Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is what shows up in that "map pack" — the three local results that appear before regular search results. If you're not there, you're invisible to people actively trying to hire you right now.

To compete in Waukesha County, your GBP needs:
- 20+ real Google reviews (this is the biggest ranking factor)
- Accurate service areas (Waukesha, Brookfield, Menomonee Falls, Pewaukee, etc.)
- Regular posts (even once a month helps)
- Photos of your truck, your team, your work
- Answered Q&A section

We manage Google Business Profiles for local contractors as part of our Core + SEO plan. Most clients see a measurable improvement in their map pack position within 60 days.

## 2. Stop Losing Calls You Can't Answer

This one is huge and almost nobody does it.

You're under a sink at 2pm. Your phone rings. You can't answer. The caller hangs up and calls the next plumber on Google.

You just lost a job — and you'll never know it happened.

**Missed-call text-back** fixes this. When you miss a call, the system automatically sends a text from your number within 30 seconds:

> "Hey! Sorry I missed your call — I'm on a job. What can I help you with? I'll call you back within the hour."

Most callers text back immediately. You keep the lead.

For a plumber doing emergency service calls, this alone can recover 5–10 leads a week that would otherwise disappear. At $300 average service call, that's real money.

## 3. Your Website Has One Job

Your website doesn't need to be fancy. It needs to do three things:

1. Load fast on mobile (most people are searching on their phone)
2. Make it stupidly easy to call you (clickable phone number, front and center)
3. Tell Google what you do and where you do it (local SEO)

A lot of plumbers have websites that look okay but fail on all three. Slow to load, phone number buried, no local keywords. If your site isn't pulling in leads, this is usually why.

## 4. Reviews Are the New Word of Mouth

When a homeowner gets a recommendation from a neighbor, they still Google the plumber before calling. And if you have 4 reviews and a competitor has 47, they're calling the competitor.

The good news: getting reviews is easier than most plumbers think. You just have to ask — right after a job, while you're still there. A text follow-up an hour later works even better.

We automate this for clients: after a job is marked complete, the system sends a text asking for a review. Our clients typically get 3–5 new reviews per month without lifting a finger.

## 5. You Don't Need Expensive Ads to Start

Google Ads and Facebook Ads can work for plumbers — but they're expensive and require constant management. For a plumbing business in Waukesha that's trying to grow steadily, we recommend building the organic foundation first:

- Get your GBP to the top of the map pack
- Set up a website that ranks for local keywords
- Get your review count up
- Install the missed-call text-back so no lead falls through

Once that engine is running, paid ads become a multiplier — not a crutch.
