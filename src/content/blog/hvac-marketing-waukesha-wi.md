HVAC is a seasonal business, which means your marketing has to work year-round — even when the phones go quiet between seasons. The contractors who stay busy in April and October are the ones who built their online presence in the months before.

Here's the marketing system that keeps HVAC companies in Waukesha County booked.

## Why HVAC Is a Perfect Fit for Local Search

When someone's AC dies in July or their furnace goes out in January, they don't scroll through Facebook. They grab their phone and search "HVAC contractor Waukesha" or "AC repair near me." They call one of the first three results. If that's not you, they're calling someone else.

HVAC is urgent, high-ticket, and heavily search-driven. That makes local SEO and Google Business Profile the highest-ROI marketing channels for any HVAC company in the Milwaukee metro.

## The Google Map Pack: Where HVAC Calls Come From

The map pack — the three local business listings that appear at the top of Google above all other results — is where the majority of local HVAC calls originate. Getting into those three spots requires:

**Reviews.** Google uses review count and recency as major ranking factors. An HVAC company with 50 reviews will nearly always outrank one with 10, even if the 10-review company does better work. The goal: get to 25+ reviews as fast as possible, then keep them coming.

**Service area targeting.** Your GBP service area should cover every city and suburb you actually serve — Waukesha, Brookfield, Menomonee Falls, Pewaukee, New Berlin, Muskego, and others. Each location you add extends your potential map pack ranking.

**Profile completeness.** Business categories, services list, photos of your trucks and team, answers to common questions, regular posts. Most HVAC companies leave half their profile blank. The ones who fill it in rank higher.

## Never Miss an Emergency Call

HVAC emergency calls are high-urgency and high-value — a customer with no AC on a 90-degree day or no heat in January isn't price-shopping. They're calling whoever responds first.

If you miss that call, they're not leaving a voicemail. They're calling the next contractor.

**Missed-call text-back** sends an automatic response within 30 seconds:

> "Hey! Sorry I missed you — I'm on a service call right now. What's going on with your system? I'll call you back as soon as I'm done."

The homeowner texts back with their issue. You have the lead, their number, and context on the problem before you even call them back.

For HVAC contractors during peak season, this system can recover 8–12 leads per week that would otherwise go cold.

## Building Reviews on Autopilot

Happy HVAC customers forget to leave reviews. They mean to, but life gets in the way.

The fix: an automated text after every completed job.

> "Hey [Name] — thanks for calling us out today! If you have a quick second, a Google review would really help our small business: [Google Review link]. Takes about 60 seconds. Thanks! — [Your name]"

Most clients get 4–8 new reviews per month after setting this up. That compounds over time — 50 reviews, then 100, then you're untouchable in your local market.

## Seasonal Marketing: Don't Go Dark in the Slow Season

The biggest mistake HVAC companies make: they stop marketing when the phone slows down. That's exactly backwards.

Spring and fall are when people **aren't** in emergency mode — which means they're open to scheduled maintenance, tune-ups, and planning ahead. That's when you want your content and SEO working hardest.

A few blog posts targeting "HVAC tune-up Waukesha" or "furnace maintenance before winter Milwaukee" can drive a steady stream of non-emergency leads in the shoulder seasons.
