If you've ever tried to get a quote for a small business website, you already know the problem: prices are all over the map. One agency quotes you $500. Another quotes $15,000. And you have no idea what you actually need.

Here's a straight answer — no fluff, no upsell.

## What a Basic Website Actually Costs

For a simple, professional small business website in Waukesha or the Milwaukee area, you're typically looking at one of three paths:

**DIY (Wix, Squarespace): $150–$400/year**
You build it yourself using a template. It'll look okay, but it won't be built for local search and you'll spend more time fighting the platform than running your business.

**Freelancer: $800–$3,000 one-time**
Better quality, but often no ongoing support. When something breaks or you need an update, you're on your own or paying hourly.

**Local agency: $1,500–$5,000 one-time + monthly retainer**
Professional-grade site built with local SEO in mind, ongoing support, hosting, and updates included. This is the route that actually gets your phone ringing.

## What You're Really Paying For

A website isn't just a digital business card. If it's built right, it's your best salesperson — working 24/7, never calling in sick, never missing a follow-up.

The real value is:
- **Local SEO** — showing up when someone in Waukesha searches for your service
- **Speed** — Google penalizes slow websites. So do customers.
- **Mobile design** — over 70% of local searches happen on phones
- **Clear calls to action** — every page should be pushing the visitor toward a call or form fill

A $500 template website usually has none of this built in.
