## What Is Missed-Call Text-Back?

When someone calls your business and you don't answer, the system automatically sends them a text within 30 seconds:

> "Hey! Sorry I missed your call — I'm on a job right now. What can I help you with? I'll get back to you within the hour. — [Your Name], [Business Name]"

That's it. Simple, fast, automatic.

The caller gets an immediate response instead of silence. Most people text back. You get the lead in writing — name, number, what they need — and you call them back when you have a free minute.

## Why It Works So Well for Contractors

**You're always on a job.** HVAC techs, plumbers, electricians, roofers — you can't answer every call while you're working. That's not a failure of your business, it's the nature of the trade. The problem is that most businesses have no system for what happens when they miss a call.

**Callers don't leave voicemails anymore.** Under 30% of callers leave a voicemail. Everyone else just hangs up and moves on. A text is different — it's immediate, it feels personal, and it keeps the conversation alive.

**It separates you from 95% of your competition.** Call any 10 contractors in Waukesha County right now. Half won't answer. Of those who don't, maybe one sends a text back. That one is you, after you set this up.

## Real Numbers

Businesses using missed-call text-back typically see:

- 30–50% of missed calls converted to text conversations
- Response times under 2 minutes (vs. callbacks that happen "sometime today")

The ROI math is easy: if you're getting 20 missed calls a week and closing even 20% of those as jobs at $400 average — that's $1,600/week you're currently leaving on the table.
