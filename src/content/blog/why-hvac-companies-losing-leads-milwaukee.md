Running an HVAC business in the Milwaukee area is competitive. You're going up against established companies with big ad budgets, and customers are searching online first — every single time.

Here's the hard truth: most HVAC companies in Waukesha County and the greater Milwaukee area are losing jobs not because of their work quality, but because of what happens before the customer ever picks up the phone.

## The 3 Biggest Lead Problems We See

### 1. Slow Follow-Up

A study by Harvard Business Review found that companies who follow up within 5 minutes are **100x more likely to close the lead** than those who wait even an hour.

Most HVAC businesses follow up when they get around to it — which is usually hours later, sometimes the next day. By then, the customer has already called your competitor.

The fix: automated text responses the second a lead comes in. "Hey, got your message — someone from our team will call you within 15 minutes." That buys you time and shows you're responsive.

### 2. Not Showing Up on Google

When someone's AC goes out on a hot July afternoon, they're not scrolling Facebook. They're Googling "HVAC repair near me" or "AC repair Waukesha." If you're not showing up in the top 3 results or on Google Maps, you don't exist to them.

Local SEO for HVAC isn't complicated, but it has to be done right:
- Google Business Profile fully filled out with photos, hours, and services
- Your website mentioning specific cities you serve (Waukesha, Brookfield, Pewaukee, Oconomowoc)
- Regular customer reviews on Google

### 3. A Website That Doesn't Convert

Traffic without conversion is just noise. We've audited dozens of contractor websites and the same problems show up every time: phone number buried in the footer, no clear "what we do," and no reason for the visitor to act right now.

Your website should have:
- Phone number at the top of every page
- A clear headline that says what you do and who you serve
- Social proof (reviews, years in business, number of jobs done)
- A fast, simple contact form

## What This Looks Like Fixed

Picture a masonry contractor in the Waukesha area. Great work, 15 years of experience, solid referrals — but nobody can find them on Google, and half their inbound calls go to voicemail with no follow-up. They're losing jobs to competitors who rank higher and respond faster.

With a fast website, a fully optimized Google Business Profile, and a missed-call text-back automation, that contractor starts capturing the leads that were always there. The work doesn't change. The systems do.
