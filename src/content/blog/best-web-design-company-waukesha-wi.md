If you search "web design Waukesha WI," you'll find a long list of options — freelancers, big agencies, offshore teams dressed up with a local address. Most of them will make you a decent-looking website. Very few will build one that actually brings in customers.

Here's what actually matters when choosing a web design company in Waukesha.

## 1. Do They Know Local SEO?

A website that can't be found on Google is a digital business card nobody reads. Any serious web design company in Wisconsin should be building every site with local search in mind from day one — proper Google Business Profile integration, local schema markup, keyword targeting for your specific city and service.

Ask them: "How will my site rank for '[my service] in Waukesha'?" If they don't have a real answer, move on.

## 2. Are They Building for Mobile?

Over 60% of local searches happen on a phone. If your site loads slow, looks cramped, or buries the phone number on mobile — you're losing jobs before you even get a call. A good Waukesha web designer will demo your site on a phone before handing it over.

## 3. Do They Handle Hosting and Ongoing Support?

The "set it and forget it" website doesn't exist. Things break. Google updates its algorithm. Your hours change. You need someone who picks up the phone when something goes wrong — not someone who handed you a WordPress login and disappeared.

## 4. What's the Real Total Cost?

Watch out for low upfront prices with hidden monthly fees. Hosting, SSL, domain, email, plugin licenses — these all add up. Get a clear number for what you'll pay month over month.

At North Star Digital, we're transparent: **Starter plan is $147/month**. That covers your website, hosting, basic SEO, and a contact form that sends leads to your phone. No surprises.

## 5. Can They Show You Results?

Any agency worth hiring should be able to show you local businesses they've helped rank, sites they've built that generate calls. Ask to see examples in your industry. Ask what happened after they launched.
