Most small business owners know their website isn't great. But they put off fixing it because it feels expensive, complicated, or "not urgent." 

Here's the thing: every month you wait is leads going to your competitors.

Here are 5 signs it's time for a new website — and what to do about it.

## 1. You're Embarrassed to Share the Link

If you hesitate before giving out your website address, that's your gut telling you something. Your website is your first impression for every potential customer who searches you online. If you wouldn't hand a prospect a brochure that looked like yours, you shouldn't have that website.

## 2. It Doesn't Work on Mobile

In 2026, over 70% of local searches happen on a phone. If your site isn't mobile-friendly — if people have to pinch and zoom, or buttons are too small to tap — you're losing those customers the second they land on your page.

Google also ranks mobile-friendly sites higher. So a bad mobile experience hurts you twice: with customers and with search engines.

## 3. You're Not Getting Leads From It

A website that doesn't generate leads is just a bill. If your phone isn't ringing from your website, it's not doing its job. A well-built site should have clear calls to action, a fast contact form, and a phone number visible on every page.

Ask yourself: when did my website last bring in a customer? If you can't remember, that's your answer.

## 4. It Takes More Than 3 Seconds to Load

Slow websites kill conversions. Studies show 53% of mobile visitors leave a site that takes longer than 3 seconds to load. And slow sites rank lower on Google.

You can test your speed for free at [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev). If your score is below 70, it's a problem.

## 5. It Was Built More Than 3 Years Ago

Web standards change fast. A website from 2021 or earlier is likely missing security updates, modern SEO structure, and the design expectations customers have today. If it was built on Wix or GoDaddy builder, it's also probably slow and hard to rank on Google.
