If you're a masonry or concrete contractor in Wisconsin, you've probably gotten most of your business through referrals. That's a great sign — it means your work speaks for itself.

But referrals have a ceiling. And that ceiling gets lower every year as more homeowners find contractors on Google before they ask a neighbor.

Here's the thing: the average masonry company in Waukesha County has almost no online presence. No website, no Google Maps reviews, no photos of their work. If you're in that group, you're invisible to a large and growing number of customers — customers who are actively searching for exactly what you do.

The good news is that means the competition is low. Right now, it doesn't take much to rank at the top of Google for masonry searches in your area.

## Who Is Searching for Masonry Contractors Online?

The customers who search Google for a masonry contractor tend to be:

- Homeowners planning a project (patio, walkway, driveway, retaining wall, tuckpointing) and comparing options
- Property managers looking for a reliable contractor they can call repeatedly
- General contractors who need a masonry sub for a larger project

These aren't tire-kickers. Someone searching "masonry contractor Waukesha" has a specific project in mind and is close to making a decision. If you're the business they find, you win.

## Why Most Masonry Businesses Aren't Getting These Leads

### 1. No Website (or a Website That Doesn't Work)

A website without photos of your work is almost useless. Masonry is a visual trade — homeowners want to see what you can do before they call. If your website doesn't have clear photos of completed projects, you're missing the entire point.

An even bigger problem: most masonry contractor websites have no phone number visible above the fold, no clear explanation of what areas they serve, and no reason for the visitor to act right now. The customer bounces, and your competitor gets the call.

What a masonry contractor website actually needs:
- Hero section with a strong photo of your best work and your phone number
- "Areas we serve" section listing every city in Waukesha County and Milwaukee metro
- Photo gallery of 10–20 completed projects (before and after where possible)
- A simple contact form
- Clear statement of what you do (concrete, brick, block, tuckpointing — be specific)

### 2. No Google Business Profile

If your business isn't on Google Maps, you don't exist for mobile searchers — and that's most of them. Google Maps is where someone goes when they want someone local, fast.

Getting on Google Maps requires a verified Google Business Profile. Setting one up is free. But to actually rank, you need:
- Complete business information (hours, phone, service area, services offered)
- At least 5–10 genuine customer reviews
- Photos of your work added consistently over time
- A website linked to the profile

Most masonry contractors in the area either have no profile, or have one that's half-finished and never updated.

### 3. No System for Following Up on Leads

When a potential customer calls and you don't answer, what happens next? For most contractors: nothing. The customer calls the next business on the list.

A missed call is a missed job. And in masonry, one job can be worth $3,000, $8,000, or $20,000 — the math on fixing this is obvious.

The fix is an automated missed-call text-back. The second you miss a call, the system sends an automatic text: "Hey, sorry I missed you — I'm on a job right now. I'll call you back within the hour. What can I help you with?" That text buys you time, keeps the customer engaged, and converts a missed call into a real conversation.

## What Ranking on Google Actually Looks Like for Masonry

Let's say you do brick patio installations in the Waukesha area. Your goal is to show up when someone searches:

- "brick patio contractor Waukesha"
- "masonry contractor near me"
- "concrete driveway installation Waukesha WI"
- "tuckpointing Waukesha"

For searches like these, there are usually two types of results:
1. **The Google Maps pack** — three local businesses with star ratings at the top
2. **Organic results** — websites ranked below the map pack

The map pack drives the majority of clicks for local searches. To get there, you need a complete GBP with reviews and a website that backs it up.

The organic results are driven entirely by your website — how well it's written, how it's structured, and whether it's been around long enough to earn Google's trust.

The fastest route for a new or small masonry business is the GBP. Build that first, get reviews, then work on the website. Within 60–90 days of consistent effort, showing up in your local area is achievable — especially if the competition is thin, which it is for most masonry contractors in Waukesha County right now.
